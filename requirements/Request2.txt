1. PHAN TICH THONG KE DATASET 2.100 ANH TU FILE JSON CUA PYNQ-Z2
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Nhan file `benchmark_2200_resume.json` tu ban chay bo phan cung.
- Viet script Python `analyze_kaggle_benchmark.py` de tinh toan:
    * Mean FPGA PSNR +/- Std (Do lech chuan).
    * Mean FPGA SSIM +/- Std (Do lech chuan).
    * Mean Bicubic PSNR +/- Std va Mean Bicubic SSIM +/- Std.
    * Mean PSNR Gain +/- Std (Gain = PSNR_FPGA - PSNR_Bicubic).
    * Ty le phan tram so luong anh co PSNR Gain > 0.
- Ve bieu do Histogram phan bo PSNR Gain va phan bo SSIM tren toan bo 2.100 anh (DPI 300).

2. KIEM THU BIT-ACCURATE DV SCOREBOARD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Xay dung Golden Model bang Python mo phong dung 100% so hoc RTL:
    * Dinh dang Pixel vao: S7.0 (8-bit signed [-128, 127], offset -128 tu goc [0, 255]).
    * Dinh dang Trong so : S0.7 (8-bit signed [-1.0, +0.992]).
    * Tich luy          : S24.7 (32-bit signed).
    * Dich bit & Kep bien: Dinh acc >> 7 sau moi tang Conv. Layer 1/2 qua ReLU kep [0, 127]. Layer 3 cong 128 va kep [0, 255].
- Chay Scoreboard kiem tra cheo tung pixel giua output FPGA va Golden Model:
    * Xac nhan sai so tuyet doi |I_FPGA - I_Golden| == 0 tren 100% diem anh.

3. DO DAC THUC NGHIEM DA NEN TANG (CPU vs GPU vs FPGA)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Viet script do thoi gian chay End-to-End tren may tinh (gom Bicubic 2x + Conv + Luu ket qua):
    * CPU: Intel/AMD (PyTorch Float32, MKL-DNN, do package power bang Intel Power Gadget).
    * GPU: NVIDIA (PyTorch CUDA Float32, do cong suat bang nvidia-smi).
    * Chay 10 lan warm-up, lay gia tri trung binh va do lech chuan qua 100 lan lap.
- Ket hop so lieu thuc te tren bo PYNQ-Z2:
    * Total SoC Latency: 444.190 ms (Khung 1024x1024), cong suat 1.438 W.
    * Throughput toan chip: 2.25 FPS, hieu qua nang luong: 1.56 FPS/W.
- Lap bang so sanh da bien: Platform | Data Type | Latency E2E (ms) | Power (W) | FPS/W.
