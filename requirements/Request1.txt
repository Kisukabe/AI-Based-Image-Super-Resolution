1. HUAN LUYEN VA DO SO LIEU FLOAT32 BASELINE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Xay dung 2 mang PyTorch Float32 tren tap anh X-quang (NIH Chest X-ray):
    a) SRCNN goc (1 -> 64 -> 32 -> 1): 8.129 tham so (Lam baseline so sanh).
    b) Compact SRCNN (1 -> 16 -> 8 -> 1): 1.649 tham so (Khop voi RTL).
       * Layer 1: Conv2d(1, 16, kernel_size=9, padding=4) + ReLU (1.296 w, 16 b)
       * Layer 2: Conv2d(16, 8, kernel_size=1, padding=0) + ReLU (128 w, 8 b)
       * Layer 3: Conv2d(8, 1, kernel_size=5, padding=2) (200 w, 1 b)
- Huan luyen voi ham Loss: Loss = MSE + 0.1 * Sobel_Edge_Loss.
- Do dac chi so chat luong Float32 tren tap test:
    * PSNR trung binh va SSIM trung binh cua SRCNN goc.
    * PSNR trung binh va SSIM trung binh cua Compact SRCNN.
    * PSNR trung binh khi chi phong to bang Bicubic (Bicubic Baseline).

2. XAY DUNG PIPELINE MO PHONG SUY THOAI VAT LY (DATA DEGRADATION)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Mo hinh toan hoc suy thoai:
    I_lr = (I_hr (*) k) \downarrow_s + n
  Trong do:
    * I_hr: Anh goc High-Resolution Ground Truth.
    * k   : Ham lam mo quang hoc Gaussian blur (sigma tu 0.5 den 1.5).
    * \downarrow_s: Ha mau khong gian 2x.
    * n   : Nhieu cam bien y te Poisson-Gaussian.
- Xuat tap du lieu cap anh (LR va HR) de ban DV va ban Nhúng lay lam tap test chung.

3. TRICH XUAT SO LIEU VA HINH ANH CHO BAI BAO
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Luu tham so train: Optimizer (Adam), Learning Rate, Batch Size, Epochs.
- Xuat do thi hoi tu ham Loss (Train Loss vs Val Loss) chuan DPI 300.
- Tong hop bang so lieu so sanh 3 muc: Bicubic vs SRCNN goc Float32 vs Compact SRCNN Float32.
